Scholarship - Introduction

Gowri Seva Sangam is a sacred social welfare initiative inspired by the divine grace of Sri Gowri Parameswari Devi, dedicated to serving humanity through education. Rooted in devotion, service, and community upliftment, the Sangam works for the welfare of the Gavara Community and other communities, especially supporting students from poor and economically weaker families.

With the blessings of the Divine Mother, Gowri Seva Sangam strives to remove financial barriers and empower meritorious students to pursue higher education and fulfil their aspirations with dignity and confidence.

In addition to providing financial assistance, the Sangam also organises felicitation programmes for top-ranking students who may not be availing scholarships or financial aid. By honouring their academic excellence, these programmes create positive role models and motivation for the younger generation.

Through financial support, recognition of merit, and a strong culture of encouragement, Gowri Seva Sangam aims to nurture a future generation that is knowledgeable, confident, and committed to the progress of society.

Vision

To create an enlightened and self-reliant Gavara community by nurturing talented students through compassionate financial support, guided by the values of devotion, service, and social responsibility under the divine blessings of Sri Gowri Parameswari Devi.

Mission

  • To provide minimum yet meaningful financial assistance to deserving and economically weaker students of the Gavara Community.
  • To encourage higher education in Medical, Engineering, and Science disciplines at state and national levels.
  • To promote academic excellence, ethical values, and social upliftment through sustained educational support.
  • To ensure transparency, fairness, and devotion-driven service in the scholarship selection and distribution process.

Scholarship Coverage

The scholarship is extended to students pursuing the following professional and technical courses approved by Gowri Seva Sangam.

  • Medical Sciences: MBBS, BDS
  • National-Level Institutions: IITs, NITs
  • State & Central Entrance-Based Programs: EAMCET, ECET, IIIT, POLYCET
  • Other recognised Science and Technology programs as approved by Gowri Seva Sangam.

Note:

  • The scholarship amount is granted once per academic year.
  • Eligible students may receive the scholarship every year until completion of their course, subject to successful renewal and verification.
  • Final approval rests with the Gowri Seva Sangam Scholarship Committee.
  • Failure to submit renewal documents within the stipulated time may result in discontinuation of the scholarship.

Application Timeline

  • Application Period: Every year from August to mid-September
  • Application Closing: Before Dasara / Navaratri
  • Scholarship Distribution: During the auspicious Navaratri celebrations

Scholarship Award Ceremony

Shortlisted scholars will be formally invited during the Navaratri days, where the scholarship will be graciously awarded by a Chief Guest of prestigious stature, representing a respected position at the government or institutional level.

This sacred occasion is conducted in a devotional atmosphere, seeking the divine blessings of Sri Gowri Parameswari Devi for the students’ academic journey and future success.

Duration of Scholarship

  • The scholarship is granted once per academic year.
  • Eligible students will receive support every academic year until completion of their enrolled program, subject to successful renewal and verification.

Application Procedure

A. Fresh Applicants

Students applying for the scholarship for the first time must submit:

  1. Duly filled Scholarship Application Form
  2. College / Institution ID Card or Bonafide Certificate / Joining Letter
  3. Admission Fee Paid Slip / Receipt
  4. Relevant Entrance Examination Rank / Allotment Details
  5. Any other documents as specified by Gowri Seva Sangam

B. Renewal Applicants

Students shortlisted in their first year and continuing in subsequent years must:

  1. Submit the Renewal Application Form
  2. Provide original pass certificates of the previously completed academic year
  3. Maintain satisfactory academic progress and discipline

Failure to submit renewal documents within the stipulated time may result in discontinuation of the scholarship.

Selection Process

  • Applications are scrutinized based on economic background, academic merit, and course eligibility.
  • Final selection rests with the Gowri Seva Sangam Scholarship Committee.
  • The decision of the committee shall be final and binding.

Gowri Seva Sangam humbly dedicates this initiative at the lotus feet of Sri Gowri Parameswari Devi, praying that her divine grace illuminates the lives of young scholars and enables them to serve society with knowledge, compassion, and integrity.
