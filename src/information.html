Monthly Regular Events

Sri Gauri Parameswarula Oonjala Seva on the third Friday of every month.

Sri Ayyappa Swamy Oonjala Seva on the first Wednesday of every month.

Every month on Ekadashi, there is a half-day Ekaham in the Sri Krishna temple.

Sankashtahara Chaturthi, Masa Shivaratri, and Pournami Pujas are performed every month.
