Puja & Timings

Daily / Weekly Pujas

Every Day

Archana • 7:30 AM

Bhaktulu mariyu kutumbaala kosam prathyeka archana nirvahinchabadindi.

Sunday

Surya Namaskaralu, Abhishekam & Soura Homam

Aarogyam, vijayam, shakti mariyu sreyassu kosam suryabhagavaanuni aashissulanu pondae nimittam prathyeka suryanamascaralu, abhishekam mariyu soura homam nirvahinchabaddayi.

Monday

Special Abhishekam

Aarogyam, shanti mariyu daivika aashissula kosam, pavitra aacharalu, prarthanalu mariyu harathitho shivuniki prathyeka abhishekam nirvahinchabadindi.

Tuesday

Subrahmanya Swami Abhishekam

Dhairyam, vijayam, sreyassu mariyu daivika aashissula kosam, pavitramaina aacharalu mariyu prarthanalatho subrahmanya swamiki prathyeka abhishekam nirvahinchabadindi.

Wednesday

Ayyappa Puja

Aarogyam, anandam mariyu adhyatmika sreyassu kosam bhajanalu, harathi mariyu prathyeka prarthanalatho kudina bhaktipurvaka ayyappa swami pooja.

Friday

Kumkuma Puja

Samruddhi kosam gowri deviki ankitham chesina prathyeka pooja.

Saturday

Ramalayam & Navagraha Puja

Daivika aasisulu, santhi, sreyassu, rakshana mariyu sarvatomukha kshemam kosam prathyeka srirama mariyu navagraha poojalu nirvahinchabaddayi.

Monthly Pujas

Every Full Moon

Pournami Puja

Pavitra pournami poojaa karyakramaalu, prathyeka alankaaram mariyu harathitho nirvahinchabadatayi.

Every New Moon

Amavasya Puja

Pitrudevatala aasisulu, shanti kosam bhaktipurvaka praardhanalu.

Monthly

Sankatahara Chaturthi

Addankulanu tolaginchukovadaniki ganapathi pooja chestaru.

Every 3rd Friday of the Month

Uyyala Seva

Sukha samtoshaalu, aishwaryam mariyu kutumba sreyassu kosam ammavari divya aashissulanu pondhenduku, bhaktipoorvakamgaa prathyeka 'uyyala seva' nirvahinchabadindi.

Every Month (Before Pournami)

Masa Shivaratri & Ekadashi Bhajana

Santhi, samruddhi mariyu adhyatmika sreyassu kosam siva bhagavaanudi mariyu vishnu bhagavaanudi aashissulanu pondae uddesamto, bhaktipurvaka prarthanalu mariyu bhajanalatho 'masa sivaratri abisekam' mariyu 'ekadashi bhajana'

Ratha Saptami

Ratha Saptami Puja

Aarogyam, vijayam, sreyassu mariyu adhyatmika sankshemam kosam aashissulanu pondae nimittam surya narayana swamiki prathyeka pooja, abhishekam mariyu prarthanalu nirvahinchabaddayi.

Yearly Pujas

Yearly Festival

Maha Shivaratri

Ratranta saage siva poojalu mariyu bhaktipurvaka namasmaranalu

Annual Festival

Sri Rama Navami

Sriramudiki ankitham cheyabadina prathyeka vedukalu.

9 Days Festival

Dussehra Navaratri Mahotsavam

Alankaram, samskruthika kaaryakramaalu mariyu annadanamto kudina ghana vedukalu.am.

Special Annual Occasion

Rukmini Sametha Radha Krishna Kalyanam

Prema, samarasyam, sreyassu mariyu kutumba kshemam kosam aashissulanu pondae nimitham, veda vidhulatho sri radhakrishnulu mariyu rukmini devila divya kalyana mahotsavam nirvahinchabadindi.

Karthika Masam

Lakshmi Narayana & Shiva Parvathula Kalyanam

Sri lakshmi narayana swami mariyu sri siva parvathi ammavarla divya kalyana mahotvam, sreyassu, dampatya samarasyam, shanti mariyu adhyatmika kalyanam kosam aashissulanu pondae uddeshamtho vaidika vidhulatho nirvahinchabadindi.

Magha Masam

Sri Surya Narayana Swamy Puja

Magha maasamlo sri surya narayana swamiki prathyeka pooja, abhishekam mariyu prarthanalu nirvahistaru; veeti dwara uthama aarogyam, vijayam, aishwaryam mariyu sanukula shakti kosam aa swami aashissulanu pondavachu.

Special Pujas

Sahasra Nama Archana

Adhyatmika sreyassu mariyu aashissula kosam daivanaamaalanu pratyekamga japinchadam.

Chandi Homam

Mukyamaina adhyatmika sandarbhalalo nirvahinche saktivantamaina homam.

Makara Jyoti Vedukalu

Ayyappa swami anugraham kosam prathyeka poojalu, bhakti bhajanalu, harati, adhyatmika karyakramalatho makara jyothini ghananga jarupukunnaru.

Siddhi Buddhi Kalyanam

(Bhadrapada Masam)
Gnanam, sreyassu, vijayam mariyu santhoshala kosam aashissulanu pondae nimitham, veda vidhulatho nirvahinche ganesuni divya kalyana mahotsavam (siddhi mariyu buddhulato).

Subrahmanya Swamy Kalyanam

(Margashira Masam)
Dhairyam, sreyassu, manchi aarogyam mariyu kutumba kshemam kosam aashissulanu pondae nimitham, veda vidhulatho sri subrahmanya swami vaari divya kalyana mahotsavam nirvahinchabadindi.
