Festivals & Annadanam

Our temple celebrates various spiritual festivals throughout the year with devotion, cultural traditions, and annadanam services for devotees.

Sudha ekadasi

Celebrated geetha mandiram ekadasi

Prathi sudha ekadashi, sri gowri geetha mandiram ekadasi bhojanalu (annadanam) bhakti mariyu bhaktito nirvahistundi. bhaktulu prathyeka praardhanalu, bhajanalu mariyu pavitramaina prasadanni sweekarinchadaaniki gumigudaaru, adhyatmika ikyatha mariyu sevanu pempondinchukuntaru.

Annadanam Service

Free prasadam and meals are served on the day Geetha Mandiram ekadasi.

Dussehra Navaratri Mahotsavam

Celebrated for 9 Divine Days

Navaratri ee aalayamlo ento vaibhavamga jarupukune pandugalalo okati. ee samayamlo prathyeka alankaralu, samskruthika karyakramaalu, bhakti sangeetham mariyu nitya poojalu nirvahistaru. ee pavitra sandarbhamlo veladi mandi bhaktulu aalayaanni sandarsistaru.

Annadanam Service

Free prasadam and meals are served daily on the day Dasami.

Subramaanya sasti

Subramanya Shasti Mahotsavam

Subrahmanya shashti parvadinaanni puraskarinchukuni, subrahmanya swami aalayamlo ento bhaktisradhalatho vedukalu nirvahistaru. ee sandarbhamga prathyeka abisekaalu, archanalu, kumkuma poojalu, bhakti prarthanalu mariyu bhajanalu jarugutayi. bhaktulu aarogyam, aiswaryam, dhairyam pondaalani mariyu aatamkala nundi rakshana labhinchalani korukuntu ee vedukallo palgontaru.

Annadanam Service

Devotees are offered prasadam and food throughout the afternoon during the festival celebrations.

Dussehra to Sankranthi – Makarajyothi Annadanam

Ayyappa Swamulu Annadanam Seva

Dasara nundi sankranthi varaku, pavitra yatranu chepatte ayyappa swamulaku ee aalayamlo annadanam nirvahistaru. makarajyoti kalamlo, bhaktulaku ento bhaktipoorvakamgaa poustikamaina prasadam mariyu bhojananni andistaru; idi niswardha seva, aathidhyam mariyu adhyatmika ikyatha patla alayaniki unna nibaddhatanu pratibimbistundi.

Annadanam Service

Free meals and prasadam are served daily to Ayyappa Swamulu from Dussehra until Sankranthi, especially during the sacred Makarajyothi season.
