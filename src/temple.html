Temples at Sri Gowri Parameswari Devalayam

Sri Ganapati Alayam Sannidhi

Om Gam Ganapataye Namaha. “Aadipoojyo Ganaadhipaha” anu aryoktini anusarinchi sakala devataaradhanalo munduga poojinchalsinadi ganapatine. Anducheta mana Gauri Panchayatana devata poojalalo munduga aa ganapatini pratishthincharu. Siddhi, Buddhi sameta Varasiddhi Vinayakuniga anekamandi bhaktula cheta poojalandukuntunnadu. Anakapallilo bellam chala prasiddhi. Atuvanti bellamunu naivedyamuga samarpinchi, mudupu katti, poojalu, abhishekalu chesi, manchi phalitalu pondutunnaru bhaktulu.

Special Programs :

  • Prati Budhavaram Panchaloha Ganapathi Utvamurtiki abisekaalu, archanalu.
  • Prati nelalo okasari vache “sankatahara chathurthi” ki vishesha panchaamrutha abisekaalu, tadanantaram lakshmiganapathi homamulu.
  • Prati nelalo hasthaa nakshatram rojuna ganapathiki aavarana archana.
  • “Vinaayaka navaratri utvalu” utvanantaram “siddhi, buddhi sametha vinayaka kalyanamu.”

Sri Shankaralayam Sannidhi

Abhishekam Eeshwarunaku chaala preethi. “Aishwaryam Eeshwaraadityeth” anu aaryokthini anusarinchi mana devalayamlo Shankaruniki raagi chembutho neeru abhishekam sampradayanga vasthoo abhishekam chesevariki aishwarya praapthini kalugajesthoo ee Gowri Parameshwarudu andarikee varalu kuripisthoo unnaadu. “Nabhomaasyandu vaasare” ani rishi vaakyamunu paatisthoo, Shraavanamaasa Somavaaraalu vishesha abhishekaalu jaripinchukuntoo bhakthulanu anugrahisthunnaaru. Kaartheeka maasamlo Shivaabhishekaalu, Rudrabhishekaalu, nomulu, vrathaalu, dhwajasthambha Kaartheeka deepaalu, Shivanaama sankeerthanalalo enthagaano Shiva bhakthulu paalgontaaru.

Special Programs :

  • Srati somavaram udayam nundi madhyahnam varaku abisekaalu, tarvata mahanyasa poorvaka rudrabisekaalu.
  • Saayantram rajata kavachamuto swami vishesha darsanam.
  • Prati nelalo vachu aarudra nakshatram roju vishesha rudrabisekaalu, homaalu, rudra pasupata homaalu.
  • Masa sivaratri roju pradoshakalamlo “sampurna rudrabisekaalu”.
  • “Shanitrayodashi nadu” udayam ekavarabisekaalu.
  • mahasivaratri nadu ratranta rudra namaka parayanalato abisekaalu, nomulu, vrathalu, om namah shivaya ekaham.

Sri Gowri Parameswari Devi Sannidhi

Maangalya devathaga prasiddhi chendina Gowri Deviki Mangala, Shukra vaaraalalo archana sarvadaa mangalapradham. Sthreeyulaku maangalya doshaalu lekundaa sakala sowbhaagya devathaga prathishtaancharu ee mana Gowri Devini. Evvaroo, ekkadaa archinchani, poojinchani vidhamugaa Shraavanamaasam nelarojulu ammavariki poojalu jaripinchadam sthaanika sampradhayam. Wyvaahika jeevitham baagundaali annaa, bhaaryaabharthala madhya anyonyatha undaalanna, manchi gunamu aarogyamulu gala santhaanam kalagaali annaa, ikkada Gowri Devi aaraadhana valane saadhyamu. Anthati goppa chaithanya moorthi mana Gowri Devi.

Special Programs :

  • Pratiroju lalitha parayana poorvaka kunkumarchana.
  • Prati mangala, shukravaralalo sri chakra abisekaalu.
  • Prathi pournamiki sri chakra navaa varanaarchana aavarana devatala homamulu.
  • Sravanamasam nela rojulu rojukoka nuthana vastradharana, alankarana, mahaa nivedana.
  • Aasweeyuja maasam dasara navaratri utsavaalu mahaa vidya parayana, mahaa shanti parayana homamulu.
  • Navaratrulalo rojukoka avataramlo ammavari prathyeka darsanaalu.
  • Sravana bahula vidiyanadu ammavari varshikotvamu, laksha kunkumarchana.
  • Dhanista nakshatram roju archana, aaradhana
  • Karthika suddha ekadasi rojuna sivaparvathula kalyana mahotvam.
  • Pratinela 3va sukravaram ammavari vunjala seva karyakramam.

Sri Lakshmi Narayana Sannidhi

Lakshmi anugraham streeyulaku sakala sowbhaagyadaayakam. Naaraayana darshanam sakala paapa vinaashanam. Ekakaalamlo iruvuri darshanam sarvadaa shreyaskaram. “Dhanamoolam idam jagath” ani anuta aarsha saampradaayam prakaaram, ee jagame dhanamu valana saaguthunnadhi kaavuna atuvanti dhanapraapthi kaluguta koraku sarvulaku aaraadhya dhoranilo paalaraathitho velasina “nagara” aalaya saampradaayamlo nirminchina Lakshmeenaaraayana darshanam maroka vishesham.

Special Programs :

  • Prati shanivaram pushpalankarana seva.
  • Margasira lakshmeevaralalo vishesha tiruvaradhana - narayana upanishat parayana.
  • Pratinela sravanam nakshatram roju vishesha alankarana, vishnu astotharaalu.
  • Sravanamasamlo krishnaastami divya vedukalu - kayam prasada vitarana.
  • Dhanurmasamlo pratah kaala archana, bala bhoga vitarana, tiruppavy paasura parayana - nitya harati.
  • Bhoogi roju vishesha abhishekam nuthana vastradharana.
  • Vaikuntha Door Darshan - Mukkoti Ekadashi Fasting Initiations - Govinda Maladharana.
  • Ykunta dwara darshanam - mukkoti ekadasi upavaasa deekshalu - govinda maaladharana.
  • Karthika suddha yekadasiki lakshminarayana paancharaatra aagama vidhanamulo 5 rojula kalyanam.

Sri Suryanarayana Sannidhi

“Aarogya Bhaaskaraadityeth” ani Rugvedamlo cheppina prakaaram prathyaksha daivamaina Surya aaraadhana sarvadaa aarogyapradham. Enni aishwaryamulu unnaa avi anubhavinchaalante manishiki aarogyam avasaram. Alaa prajalandaroo manishi aarogyam pondaalani uddheshamtho ee panchaayathana devalayamlo Suryanaaraayanunni prathishtaancharu. Ikkada labhinchina Surya theerthaanni sweekarinchi anaarogya shareera bhaagaalaku thaakithe aarogyam vasthundani pratheethi. Kaavuna Surya namaskaaramulu cheyuta shubhapradham.

Special Programs :

  • Aadivaram - suryabisekam, rugveda parayana, surya namascaralu
  • Magha maasamlo prathyeka poojalu.
  • Aadhasaptami - rashtra panduga vedukalu.
  • Ushaa padminee sujnaa chaya sametha surya narayana swami kalyanam.
  • Kruttikaa nakshatram ikkada suryanaarayanuni nakshatram - “agnidevudu, deepam, subrahmanya swami” vishesha “mahasauram” parayana.
  • Radhasaptami rojuna - nomulu, vrathalu, sthanika sthalaachara poojalu
  • Soura panchayatana mahayagam nirvahana.
  • “Aadhitya hrudayam” surya stotra parayana poorvaka soura, homaalu, ekadasi rojuna surya - chandrula nomulu.

Sri Geetha Mandiram Sannidhi

Shree Raadhaakrishna mandiram bhakthulaku shaanthi, aadhyaathmika aanandham mariyu sampoornatha bhaavananu kaligisthundhi. Gopikala nathsaroopini Shreeraadha Devi mariyu paramabhaktha Shreekrishnudi andhamaina vigrahaalu vaividhyabharitha pushpaalatho alankarinchi prathishtincha baddayi. Prathi roju udayam sundharaartha praarthanalu, sankeerthanalu mariyu bhakthigeethala madhya paatisthaaru; bhakthulanu prasaadhamtho aathithyamga swaagastharu. Sthaanikulatho kalisi vivaahasamayaalu, pandugalu mariyu ithara saamoohika kaaryakramaalu ee mandiramlo ghananga jaruguthaayi. Raadha-Krishna jantaku ankitham chesina uthsava kaalamlo deepaaradhanalu, bhajanalu mariyu haritho bhojana kramaalu prathyeka aakarshanaga untaayi. Saadhaaseedhaa nirmaanamlo unna ee aalayam undadam dwaara sthaanikulatho aadhyaathmika bandham gaadhamavthoo maanasiha shaanthi mariyu saamaajika aikyatha peruguthundhi.

Special Programs :

  • Kaarthika maasa shukla ekadasi nundi chaaturmaasya deeksha praarambhamavutundi.
  • Ee kaalamlo bhaktulu udayam mariyu sayantram velallo kunkumaarchanalu nirvahistaru. sayantram velallo 'lalitaa sahasranaama' parayanam, bhajanalu mariyu srikrishna paramaatmaku prathyeka sevalu jarugutayi.
  • Sravana maasa krishna ashtami rojuna, udayam 6 gantalaku sri radhakrishnula vigrahalaku abhishekam nirvahistaru. sayantram kolataalu, utti kottadam, bharatanatyam vanti samskruthika kaaryakramaalu untayi. ratri 9 gantala nundi 12 gantala varaku bhajana kaaryakramaalu jarugutayi. ardharaatri 12 gantalaku srikrishna paramaatmaku uyyala seva nirvahinchi, anantaram prasada vitarana jarugutundi.
  • Margasira maasa shukla ekadasi (gita jayamthi) sandarbhamga, edu rojula paatu 'hare raama hare krishna' naama sankeerthana jarugutundi. ee vaaram rojula kaaryakramam mugisina tarvata sri rukmini krishnula vivaha veduka (kalyanam) nirvahistaru.
  • Vvysakha masamloni ekadasi rojuna sri rukmini krishnula varshika kalyanotsavam jarugutundi.
  • Prati nelaa shukla ekadasi rojuna, bhaktulache 'hare raama hare krishna' naama sankeerthana athyantha vaibhavamga nirvahinchabadutundi.
  • Pratiroju udayam 7 gantala nundi 8 gantala varaku bhagavantuni keertanalu (bhajanalu) jarugutayi.
  • Prati yeta dhanurmasamlo, udayam 7 gantala nundi 9 gantala varaku bhaktulu sri radhakrishnulaku prathyeka poojalu nirvahistaru; ee samayamlo 'tiruppaavai' patanam kuda jarugutundi.

Sri Ayyappa Swamy Sannidi

Ayyappa Swamy Temple is a sacred shrine dedicated to Lord Ayyappa, worshipped for discipline, devotion, and self-purification. Ayyappa Deeksha is an important spiritual vow observed before pilgrimage, usually involving 41 days of strict rules, purity, prayer, and a simple way of life to prepare the devotee mentally and physically for worship.

The importance of Ayyappa Deeksha lies in its emphasis on self-control, humility, and inner cleansing, helping devotees reduce worldly distractions and focus on devotion. It is considered a path to spiritual discipline, peace of mind, and sincere surrender to Lord Ayyappa.

Sri Subrahmanya Swamy Sannidhi

Subrahmanya Swami Paramashivuni kumaarudigaa, dhairyam, gnaanam, shakthi mariyu vijayaaniki pratheekagaa poojimpabade divya devatha. Skandudu, Kaarthikeyudu, Shanmukhudu, Kumaaraswami ani kooda piluvabade ee swami, bhakthulaku shathrunivaarana, aatmavishvaasam, vidyaabhivruddhi mariyu aadhyaathmika balaanni prasaadisthaarani vishvasi-sthaaru. Nemali vaahanamgaa, shoolayudhamtho darshanamichhe swamivaaru, sarpadosha nivaaranakoo, mangalakara jeevithanikee mukhyadaivamgaa bhaavinchabadataaru. Bhakthishraddhalatho Subrahmanya Swamini smarinchadam shubhapradham.

Special Programs :

  • Prathi mangalavaram udayam gam||8:00 ni|| nundi subrahmanya swamiki, rahu kethuvulaki panchaamrutha abisekaalu, poojalu.
  • Margasira suddha shashti rojuna subrahmanya shashti sandarbhamugaa udayam gam||5:00 ni|| nundi moola virat ku vishesha abhishekaalu poojalu. gam||7:00 ni|| nundi kalyanamurtulu thiruveedhi udayam gam||10:00 ni|| nundi sri valli devasena sametha subrahmanya swamiki kalyana mahotvam. madhyahnam gam||12:00 ni|| nundi anna samaradhana. sayantram gam||7:00 ni|| nundi padipoojaa mahotvam.

Sri Ramalayam Sannidhi

Ikshvaaku vamsha thilakudaina aa Ayodhya Raamudini saparivaaramgaa aaraadhinchukovadam poorvajanma sukrutham. Andulonoo aadhi, lakshmi vaaraalalo “Raamadevuni kathaasmaranam” anirvachaneeyamaina phalitham. Seetha sametha Raama darshanam Naaraayana, Shiva naama militha adbhutha sandesham. Rojulo okasaarainaa Raavanaamasmaranam* marujanma leni moksha pradhaatha. Kalidhosha nivaaranalo Raamanaamame thaaraka manthramani vaaggeyakaarula maata. “Hare Raama Hare Raama Raama Raama Hare Hare. Hare Krishna Hare Krishna Krishna Krishna Hare Hare”.

Special Programs :

  • Prati shanivaram poolamaalalato, thulasipatritho swami poojalu.
  • “Punarvasu” nakshatra samayamlo brahmii muhurtha kaalamlo mulaviratlaku abisekam.
  • Rajata kavachamulato alankarana avanikaadarsanam.
  • Ekadashi rojuna ardham ekaham nirvahana - hare raama sankeertanaa yagnam.
  • Sriramanavamiki paancharaatra aagama vidhanamga “sitaramula kalyanam”.
  • Navaratrula nirvahana - raama pattabhishekam - pushpanjali seva.
  • Pratiroju swamivari thiruveedhi tirukkalyana sankalpa pathanam.
  • Rama sthupam nirvahana kosam “ramakoti rachana”.
  • “Ramakrathu stambham” - ramakoti sthupam.

Sri Santoshi Mata Sannidhi

Shraavana Pournami Shree Santhoshimaatha Ammavaari puttinaroju, Santhoshimaatha Ammavaaru saakshaat-thu Shree Ganapathi puthrika.   • Prati sukravaram udayam 7 gantalaku, bhaktulu ammavari samakshamlo kunkumaarchana poojanu nirvahistaru.

Navagraha

Maanavulandari shreyassuku aadhaaramu ee Navagrahamulu. Andulonoo Shani, Raahu, Kethu aaraadhana athyantha dhosha nivrutthi kaaranam. Eeshaanya bhaagamlo Shamee vruksham needalo prathishtinchabadina Navagraha devalayam pradakshinalaku, archanalaku, abhishekaalaku anukoolam. Navagraha darshanam gochaara gruha dhosha nivaaranamu. Navagraha pradakshina bhoomandala pradakshinatho samaanam. Aalaya darshanam praarambham ayyedhi ee Navagrahaala darshanamthone. Tharvaathe kshethra darshanam. Anthati visheshana Navagraha shaanthulu, bilaarishtha shaanthulu, baalaasaare shaanthulu ikkada jaripinchadam sthaanika sthalaachaara aanavaayithee.

Special Programs :

  • Shani trayodashi rojuna pratah kaalam nundi thailabisekamulu.
  • Thila, gudu, thandu lanti danamulu.
  • Shanivaram pradakshinalu, ashtoottara poojalu, tila danamulu.
  • Mangalavaram kujadosha poojalu, santhulu.
  • Guruvaram sanagalu abhisheka danamulu.
  • Rahukaala samayamlo kunkumarchanalu.
  • Budhavaram vidyabhivruddhi parikshalalo uttirnatha kosam budhagraha poojalu, vishnu sahasranama parayanalu jarugunu.

Sri Rama Koti Stupa Sannidhi

Raamakothisthoopam Shreeraama naama japam, bhakthi, mariyu aadhyaathmika samarpanaku pratheekamaina pavithra sthalam. Ikkada Raamanaama pusthakaalu bhadraparachi, shubhadhinaana vaatini therichi samarpinche sampradaayam undhi. Prathi roju bhakthulu Shreeraamuniki poojalu chesthoo bhakthibhaavaanni vyaktham chesthaaru.

Raamakothi sthoopam Shreeraama naama smaranaku, bhakthi sampradaayaniki anki-thamaina pavithra sthalam. Ikkada bhakthulu raasina Raamanaama pusthakaalanu oka prathyeka paddhathilo bhadraparusthaaru, mariyu samvathsaraaniki okasaari shubhadhinaana sthoopaanni therichi aa pusthakaalanu samarpinche aachaaram untundhi. Prathi roju bhakthulu vachhi Shreeraamuniki poojalu, deepaaradhanalu, mariyu naamasmarana chesthoo aadhyaathmika shaanthini pondhuthaaru. Raamakothi lekhanatho koodina ee sthoopam bhakthilo nibaddhatha, niyamasheelatha, mariyu Raamabhakthini prathibimbisthundhi.

Akhand Jyoti Sannidhi

Akhandajyothi sannidhi anedhi niranthara deepaaradhanatho aadhyaathmika shuddhi, shaanthi, mariyu daivasaannihithyaanni soochinche pavithra kendhram. Gowri Panchaayathana devalayaalalo idhi visheshamgaa pariganinchabaduthundhi, endhukante akhanda jyothi bhakthi nirantharathaku, sankalpa balaaniki, mariyu devalaya prathishtaku pratheekagaa nilusthundhi. Ee jyothi veluguthundadam valla aalayamlo paavana vaathaavaranam nelakoni, bhakthula manassulo vishvaasam, ekaagratha, mariyu sevaabhaavam peruguthaayi. Alaage, devalaya shreyassu, bhakthula raaka, mariyu samaajamlo aadhyaathmika aikyatha peragadaaniki idhi mukhyamgaa bhaavinchabaduthundhi.

Goshala

goshala anedi alayaniki anubandhamga unde oka pavitra sthalam, idi govulanu samrakshistundi. idi kevalam jantuvula aasrayam matrame kadu. idi gopuja, daiva seva mariyu swachchamaina jeevanasailiki prathika. aalaya pranganamlo goshala undatam bhaktulaku santini istundi. idi gopujanu mariyu seva spoorthini penchutundi. oka alayaniki goshala chala mukyamainadi. idi poojalaku avasaramaina swachchamaina palu, perugu mariyu neyyini andistundi. govulanu rakshinchadam dharmanni, dayanu mariyu samaja sreyassunu thestundani prajalu nammutaru. konni alayalu gosalanu danadharmaalu, prarthana mariyu adhyatmika sevalaku kendramga upayogistayi. mottam mida, goshala aalaya pavitratanu penche oka mukyamaina bagam. idi aalaya sevalu svachamga, sampurnamgaa mariyu dharmikamga jaragadaaniki sahayapadutundi.
